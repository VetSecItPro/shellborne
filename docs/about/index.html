About Shellborne

Shellborne is a Mac terminal for running many AI coding agents side by side, keeping track of each one, and making sure no session is lost along the way.

The name

Shellborne borrows from two military traditions. Sailors who cross the equator earn the title shellback. Paratroopers go airborne. Both mean you went through something hard and came out the other side, which is what every agent session in Shellborne should do. The icon is a turtle walking forward with a terminal prompt painted on its shell.

Who builds it

One developer, VetSecItPro, directing AI coding agents. The agents write much of the code. Every change is tested first, checked by automated gates, and approved by a person before it merges; the riskiest changes also get a review from a second AI model. No independent human security audit has happened, and the security policy says so.

What it costs

Nothing. Shellborne is free and open source under the Apache License 2.0.
